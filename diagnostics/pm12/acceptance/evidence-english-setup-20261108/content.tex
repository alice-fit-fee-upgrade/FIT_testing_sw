\section{Test procedure and criteria}
\subsection{Hardware identity and PGOOD}
Read factory FPGA DNA and MCU EEPROM serial under leases. Match enrolled UID, integrated build and stable physical PGOOD.

Limits: MCU EEPROM serial and FPGA IDCODE are not unique factory identity. CPU factory signature not read.

\subsection{JTAG chain and168 static CPLD--FPGA links}
Validate13 IDCODEs. Program checksum-verified link diagnostic into FPGA SRAM only.338 walking-one/zero vectors in each of gateware and independent boundary-scan receive paths. Restore checksum-verified integrated image, initialize private link, verify build and repeat HDMI/ADC/TDC functional tests.

Limits: Static connectivity, not at-speed margin or analog quality. Expected SYS reset from deliberate FPGA SRAM reconfiguration; never reset or program MCU/CPLD/flash.

\subsection{GTX internal PRBS, MAC loopback and external link}
Exercise GTX PRBS and MAC loopback with negative controls; restore integrated image. External PM$\leftrightarrow$Kasli private link checked separately by integrated suite.

Limits: Legacy1000BASE-X image expects old exact40MHz clock. Requalify with present40.0787MHz; old PASS is historical only.

\subsection{I2C mux address inventory and bus topology}
Single-branch scans through both muxes, address readback and decoded monitor IDs; preserve current/voltage samples.

Limits: Legacy runner programs older FPGA and restores obsolete baseline. MCU telemetry is integrated, exhaustive topology adapter still requires migration.

\subsection{FPGA configuration flash ownership and read-only access}
Verify ownership guard, PROGRAM\_B hold, JEDEC and bounded READ03, bus release, reload integrated SRAM image.

Limits: Destructive to volatile FPGA state. Qualified current-image restoration/recovery adapter required; stored-image boot remains unconfirmed.

\subsection{Power sequencing and timeout shutdown}
Observe EN-to-PG rise and timeout shutdown without replacing original MCU protection; record rails and fault state.

Limits: Fault injection and power-cycle qualification NOT\_RUN; static PGOOD is not equivalent.

\subsection{CPU peripherals, SPI walking patterns and power telemetry}
Read PING, GPIO, temperature ID, three TDC chip IDs, PLL, FPGA signature, 32 walking patterns with scratch restoration, INA and POWER. Recheck PGOOD before sensor access.

Limits: No power cycling or timeout fault injection. Sensor numeric limits require a frozen acceptance specification.

\subsection{HDMI, all digital ADC paths and stimulated TDC}
Four HDMI lanes: walking discovery and slow/fast PRBS, empty frames; SPI signature reads only. Capture both ADC banks, synthetic and physical, all12 channels. Quiet/stimulated/quiet TDC:64 pulses to configured channel. Check uptime.

Limits: TDC stimulus only on the physically connected channel. No calibrated ADC analog acceptance for unconnected channels. HDMI SPI writes disabled: bus shared with MCU.

\subsection{Gate delay versus internal DAC-A}
Establish explicit DAC-A0000 baseline;20 code points0..65535; common trigger and ISERDES phase buffers; restore0000 and verify phase return. Save every raw capture.

Limits: Sampled centroid, nominal sample metadata; no calibrated absolute phase/jitter claim. Original20MHz CPLD gate divider required.

\subsection{ADC differential transfer and adjacent data-bit coverage}
SCPI DAC differential-1.2..+1.2V, commonmode1V,257 points, ADC A/B. Decode4096-word buffers at STR edges, verify tags/generations;12 bits and11 neighbor pairs, all4 states with sufficient population. Restore1V/1V.

Limits: Requested voltage, no calibrated gain/DNL. Per-pin0..3.3V ceiling. Functional persistent-tie test, not resistance/pin-order/timing-margin proof.

\subsection{CFD analog monitor DC levels}
Read all8 monitor nodes with DMM7510, correct divider ratios, leave all shared monitor muxes inhibited.

Limits: Original script is channel12-specific. Parameterized multi-module inhibition and DMM/wiring qualification required.

\subsection{Internal DAC B/C/D transfer curves}
65 code settings perDAC, three DMM readings per setting, corrected analog-node voltage and verified explicit-code restoration.

Limits: Original script is channel12-specific. Per-channel restore codes, shared mux inhibition and DMM wiring must be configured.

Deviation is relative to the median of measured channels at common requested stimulus settings, without interpolation. With fewer than two channels this comparison is NOT COMPARABLE, rather than zero dispersion. Values are descriptive, not a calibrated gain/error acceptance limit.

Bit decisions exclude rail codes 0/4095. Both values of every bit and all four states of each neighboring pair must be populated: at least 64 samples and 2\% of all interior samples per state, plus at least 8 samples at each of 3 distinct settings. At least 16 settings, 4096 interior samples and 3000-code mean span are required for PASS.

ADC strobe validation uses the4x sampled clock model:1023 or1024 rising and falling edges, intervals3/4/5 only, cumulative rising and falling phase spans at most one sample, and pulse widths1..3 samples. The ADC word selected immediately before STR must equal its preceding sample. EDGE QUANTIZED is reported separately from ALIGNED; neither is a calibrated jitter result. A strict all4-interval check is a separate phase-alignment diagnostic.

This excludes sampled persistent stuck/tied signatures under these conditions. It does not establish electrical resistance, bit order, timing margin, ADC INL/DNL or full missing-code compliance. A sine histogram is arcsine-shaped; a calibrated continuous ramp is a separate density test.
