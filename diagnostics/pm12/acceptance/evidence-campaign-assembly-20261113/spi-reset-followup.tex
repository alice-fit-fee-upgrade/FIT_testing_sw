\section{PM HDMI parser held in reset: 14 November 2026}
Temporary PM image \texttt{8da2d6d3}, derived from the corrected-clock
\texttt{c7531465}. Only the PM HDMI parser is held in its original deselected
reset branch; this does not reset the Kasli master or MCU SPI engine.
Physical SCK still clocks the circuit. Runtime IPbus 305 selects hold 0/1;
the control is initialized at configuration but deliberately survives SYS
reset. It was read back after every block. No SYS reset occurred to demonstrate
retention physically. Invalid hold value 2 was rejected.

MISO data is forced zero while held; actual HSEL still controls its physical
output enable. Normal read 3 is primed before each block to logically match
zero MISO. Alternating normal/held trials share one PM image and unchanged
Kasli, buffer direction, OE and external clock profiles.

Nine priming reads are additional and excluded from these 132 measured frames.
\textbf{The result is inconclusive for suppression: normal controls also did
not reproduce the fault.} Resetting the parser has not been established as
a fix or the unique cause. Logical MISO matching is not a continuous electrical
pad measurement. The original source and a testbench check normal signatures,
held parser state under full frames, zero output and release/recovery.
Dell implementation: WNS 0.095 ns, WHS 0.007 ns; no DRC errors, baseline FIFO warning.

Exact \texttt{c7531465} was restored afterwards. A further 36 normal signature
frames also had zero observed decreases/timeouts. Thus the fault remains an
open intermittent observation, currently not reproduced. No clock-phase,
routing or initialization mechanism is established by this comparison.
Fresh physical ADC bankA passed all 12 channels after restoration and after
the original-image control. Power, TDC frontend 0xfff and driven Kasli SPI
were healthy. No Kasli, MCU, CPLD or flash programming or explicit external
PLL/profile changes were performed.

Raw data, exact candidate source/bitstream, timing, simulation and restoration
receipts are retained in \texttt{system/evidence-spi-reset-20261114}.
An initial preflight rejected the current image because a historical helper
hardcoded old \texttt{c7c42d28}; it stopped before programming and is retained
as rejected. The completed test uses explicit current identity and physical
DNA. Historical campaign source contracts were preserved.
