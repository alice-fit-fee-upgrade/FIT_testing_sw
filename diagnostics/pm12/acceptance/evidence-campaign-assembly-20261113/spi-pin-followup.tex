\subsection{Return MISO probe and limitations}
On the restored original Kasli, diagnostic read3 returns zero from the
retained isolated PM slave map. After warmup, 23 frames logically keep its
MISO data zero while selected. No restart was observed, but 18 ordinary
signature controls were also stable: this comparison is inconclusive.
Output still gates with SEL; no continuous electrical pad observation is claimed.
A previous programming interval may have changed the condition, but causation
or a clock-phase mechanism is not established.

The terminal ADC check found one unstable selected word on channel 1,
position 1255. Its complete raw buffer and exception are retained. A fresh
leased physical ADC bankA capture subsequently passed all 12 channels.
This is an open transient observation, not an unconditional ADC qualification.
The constant-MISO helper saved all 43 SPI trials before its final ADC exception;
its final metadata were not saved, so a separate postflight provides final state.

Two initial attempts are retained as interrupted, not negative results:
one was stopped after lengthy read timeouts, another lost a mode-write ACK.
Both restored the original image. The completed bounded series is separately
identified as \texttt{bounded03}. Raw sources/logs, candidate bitstream,
validation and postflight are in \texttt{system/evidence-spi-pins-20261114}.

The FIN control selects direction, not disable-both. No direction reversal
into an active FPGA was attempted. Kasli reload intervals interrupt outputs
and are excluded; outputs remained driven during pin trials. Final state:
original Kasli SHA \texttt{a71a08a2...}, slot 6 mask 00b0, enable 1/status 9,
PM \texttt{c7531465}, frontend 0xfff and power healthy. No external PLL,
MCU, CPLD, flash or analog settings were changed.
